# About me
{: .publication-section-title }

I am currently a senior undergraduate student in **Turing Class** (an elite undergraduate program), at [Yuanpei College](https://yuanpei.pku.edu.cn/), [Peking University](https://www.pku.edu.cn/). 

My research focuses on cryptography and privacy-preserving systems, with a particular interest in efficient secure computation. I am also interested in security and privacy challenges arising in modern AI systems.

I participated in the 2026 summer research internship program at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/), advised by Prof. [Elaine Shi](https://elaineshi.com/), where I studied and researched on function secret sharing and its applications. Prior to that, I was advised by Prof. [Mingxun Zhou](https://wuwuz.github.io/), and designed new frameworks to optimize the practical performance of private information retrieval (PIR).
